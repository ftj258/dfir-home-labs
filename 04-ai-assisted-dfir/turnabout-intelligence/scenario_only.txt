M57.biz is a small start-up. A few weeks after the company was founded, a
confidential spreadsheet listing the names, Social Security numbers, and
salaries of its employees appeared on a competitor's public website. Only
Jean's laptop held a copy of that file. Jean says she does not know how the
data left her machine and believes she was hacked. Determine how the data
was stolen — or whether Jean is less innocent than she claims.
